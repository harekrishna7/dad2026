What the regional connectivity architecture is designed to deliver.

High Availability

Resilient infrastructure engineered for continuous operation of mission-critical regional workloads.

Multi-Carrier Connectivity

Cloud Interconnection

Direct, low-latency access to leading global cloud ecosystems from the heart of Northeast India.

Enterprise Networking

Enterprise-grade networking for corporates, institutions and public-sector organisations.

Network Design Principles

The architectural foundations behind the regional connectivity vision.

>

Carrier-Neutral Architecture

An open, provider-agnostic network that keeps infrastructure choice in the customer's hands.

Multiple Licensed Telecom Providers

Diverse, licensed carrier partnerships for redundancy, competition and regulatory compliance.

>

Redundant Fiber Paths

Diverse physical routing protects against single points of failure on any corridor.

High Availability

Architectural resilience built around continuous uptime and rapid failover.

Secure Routing

Hardened routing policies and monitoring across the entire regional footprint.

Cloud-First Integration

Designed for seamless, high-performance interconnection with global cloud platforms.

>

Scalable Bandwidth

Capacity that scales on demand to match regional AI and cloud growth.

>

Zero Trust Security

Identity-first security posture applied across the whole network fabric.
