1. Who we are and scope of this policy

Dharmanagar AI DataSync Private Limited ("DADsync", "we", "us", "our") operates the website at dadsync.in (the "Website"). Our place of business is Dharmanagar, North Tripura 799250, India.

For personal data processed through the Website, DADsync is the Data Fiduciary — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing — under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act").

This policy covers personal data collected through the Website only. It does not cover:

  • personal data processed by our third-party service providers in their own capacity (their policies apply to their processing);
  • personal data processed when we deliver services to a client under a signed agreement — in those engagements the processing roles, purposes and safeguards are set out in the agreement itself, and we may act as a Data Processor rather than a Data Fiduciary;
  • personal data of our employees, contractors or job applicants once they enter our recruitment and HR records beyond the initial application received through the Website.

This policy should be read with our Website Terms & Conditions.

2. Definitions

  • Personal data — any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to that data.
  • Data Principal — the individual to whom the personal data relates (in this policy, you).
  • Data Fiduciary — the person or entity that alone or with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • Data Processor — a person or entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
  • Processing — any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing, disclosure, erasure and destruction.
  • Consent — a clear, affirmative, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of your wishes, given by a clear affirmative action, signifying agreement to the processing of your personal data for a specified purpose.
  • Data Protection Board of India — the Board constituted under the DPDP Act.

3. What personal data we collect, and how

We inspected every form on the Website. The collection is as follows, and it differs from page to page — not every page uses the same flow.

3.1 Contact form — Contact page (contact.html)

Fields: first name (required), last name (required), email address (required), telephone number (optional), interest area (a dropdown selection, for example "Colocation / Data Centre", "AI / Machine Learning", "Careers / Recruitment", "Investor Relations"), and message (required). The form also contains a hidden anti-spam field that a real visitor never fills in.

Where it goes: on submit, the values are written directly from your browser to a Google Firebase Cloud Firestore database in our Firebase project, as a document in a collection named contacts, together with a server-generated timestamp. If the database library has not loaded, or the write fails, the page instead opens your own email application with the same content pre-filled and addressed to us, and tells you that this is what happened. Nothing is saved on our side in that fallback path until you press send in your own email client.

3.2 Contact form — Home page (index.html)

Fields: name (required), telephone number (optional), email address (required) and message (required).

Where it goes: this is a different flow from the Contact page. The same values are written from your browser to our Firebase Realtime Database, in a node named contact_messages, with a timestamp and a status flag. A separate form-delivery endpoint is defined in the page's code but is not configured with a live destination, so no submission is transmitted to it in the current build. If the database write is not available, the page opens your own email application with a pre-filled draft instead.

3.3 Careers application form — Home page modal (index.html)

Fields: name (required), email address (required), telephone number (optional), a free-text note on suitability (optional), and the role applied for (set automatically from the role you clicked).

Where it goes: written from your browser to our Firebase Realtime Database, in a node named job_applications, with a timestamp and a status flag; the same fallback to your own email application applies. Please note: the form does not upload files. Any résumé or attachment is added by you in your own email client, if the fallback path opens it.

3.4 Careers page (careers.html)

There is no form that submits to a server on this page. Each "Apply Now" and "Send Your Resume" control is a link that opens your own email application with our address, a role-specific subject line and a draft body pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted to us unless and until you choose to send that email. When you do, we receive your email address, whatever you write in the message, and any attachment you add — typically a résumé, which may contain personal data such as your employment history, education, contact details and, if you include them, other categories of personal data.

3.5 Emails and telephone calls you initiate

If you email or call us at the addresses and numbers published on the Website, we receive your contact details, the content of your communication, any attachments, and — from your email headers — technical information such as your email address, the sending server and timestamps.

3.6 The AI assistant

If you use the embedded AI chat, the messages you type, and the conversation context sent with them, are transmitted to our third-party AI chat provider. See section 6.

3.7 Technical data and server logs

When you load any page, your browser requests files from our hosting and content-delivery providers. Those providers necessarily receive your IP address, the requested URL, your browser's user-agent string and the time of the request, and they may keep standard access logs under their own policies. We do not operate an analytics or advertising tracker on the Website (see section 7), and we do not ourselves build a profile of your browsing.

3.8 What we do not collect

The Website has no user accounts, no login for visitors, no payment processing, no newsletter subscription and no advertising or behavioural tracking on public pages. We do not ask for and do not want identity documents, financial account details, health data, biometric data, caste or religious information, or precise location data through any Website form. Please do not send such data through the forms.

Our Website also contains an internal administrative area used by authorised DADsync personnel, protected by Firebase Authentication. It is not part of the public Website and does not collect personal data from visitors.

6. The AI assistant and third-party AI processing

The Website embeds an AI chat assistant provided by Dify, loaded from udify.app, and also offers a full-page chat at chatbot.html. The assistant runs on Dify's infrastructure using a large language model; the model provider is owner confirmation required.

What is transmitted. When you send a message, the text you type and the surrounding conversation context are sent to the AI provider, together with technical request data. The assistant does not require an account and does not ask for your name. If you volunteer personal data in a chat message — your own or someone else's — that text leaves your browser and reaches the provider.

What we can and cannot verify. We verify that our pages load the Dify embed and open the assistant in a frame from udify.app. We can confirm that the assistant is a third-party cloud service and that chat content therefore leaves our infrastructure. We cannot, from the Website alone, verify the provider's retention period, whether chat transcripts are stored, whether they are used for model training, the sub-processors it uses, or the countries in which it processes data. Those points are owner confirmation required and must be confirmed against the provider's data-processing terms before this section can be treated as complete. We do not claim that conversations are anonymous, deleted after a set period, or excluded from model training, because we have not verified any of those things.

Our own handling. We do not build a profile of you from your chat conversations, we do not use them for marketing, and we do not use them to make automated decisions about you. Whether we retain chat transcripts in our own systems, and for how long, is owner confirmation required.

Please do not enter confidential business information, personal data of other people, credentials, financial details or any sensitive information into the AI assistant. For anything substantive, use the contact form or email us instead.

The assistant's output can be wrong. It is not professional or legal advice, and it is not a statement of our services, pricing or commitments. See section 7 of our Terms & Conditions.

7. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The public Website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and no analytics or tag-management script is loaded. We verified this by inspecting the code: no Google Analytics, no gtag, no tag manager, and no third-party tracking pixel is present on any public page.

The only storage the Website writes to your device is a single local-storage entry named dadsync-theme, which remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. It contains the words "dark" or "light" and nothing else. It is not a cookie, it is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it stays on your device until you clear your browser storage. You can remove it at any time through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the Website.

Our hosting, content-delivery and font providers may set their own strictly functional or security-related cookies or use request-level identifiers as part of delivering files; those are outside our control and are governed by their policies.

Because we run no analytics, we do not measure visitor behaviour, we do not create audience segments, and we do not track you across other websites. If analytics are enabled in future, this section and the notice on our forms will be updated before that happens; whether any analytics is planned is owner confirmation required.

8. How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, and then erase or anonymise it — subject to any legal requirement to keep it longer.

We have not fixed specific retention periods for Website enquiries and applications. Rather than state a period we cannot stand behind, we identify the practice and flag the gap:

  • Contact enquiries — retained while the enquiry or resulting relationship is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards for continuity of correspondence. A specific retention period is owner confirmation required.
  • Job applications and résumés — retained for the recruitment process for the role applied for. Whether unsuccessful applications are kept for future openings, and for how long, is owner confirmation required.
  • AI chat conversations — our own retention is owner confirmation required; the provider's retention is owner confirmation required (see section 6).
  • Emails — retained in our business mailbox for as long as needed for correspondence and record-keeping. A specific period is owner confirmation required.
  • Server and access logs — held by our providers for their own standard periods, which we have not verified. Owner confirmation required.

When a retention period ends, we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it, and require our processors to do the same where they hold it on our behalf. You may ask us to delete your data earlier — see sections 10, 11 and 12. We may need to keep limited records where the law requires it, or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, in which case we will tell you so.

9. How we protect your data

We take the security of personal data seriously, and we describe only what we can actually verify:

  • Encryption in transit. The Website is served over HTTPS, and submissions from the contact and application forms are sent to our Firebase project over encrypted connections. Access to our Firebase endpoints is restricted by the provider's transport security.
  • Restricted administrative access. The internal administrative area that lets our team read enquiries and applications is protected by individual authentication through Firebase Authentication. It is not linked from the public Website and is not reachable by visitors.
  • Data minimisation. The forms ask only for what is needed to respond: no identity documents, no financial details, no accounts.
  • Least-privilege intent. Access to enquiry data is intended to be limited to personnel who need it to respond.

We do not claim any security certification, audit attestation, accreditation, or facility-level security control — including ISO/IEC 27001 certification, SOC 2 attestation, biometric or physical access controls at a data centre, or per-tenant encryption — because we have not verified any of these for the Website's processing. Any such statement elsewhere must not be read into this policy. If any certification or control exists and should be described here, it is owner confirmation required, and should be stated with its exact scope and certificate reference.

No method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and we do not claim to. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act in accordance with our obligations under the DPDP Act, including notifying the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required. Our internal incident-response procedure, notification timelines and the person responsible are owner confirmation required.

10. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to the DPDP Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right to access — to obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you, the processing activities, and, where relevant, the identities of the Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to correction and erasure — to ask us to correct inaccurate or misleading data, to complete incomplete data, and to erase data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected for (subject to any legal retention requirement).
  • Right to know — to know, in a concise and clear manner, what personal data we process and the manner in which we process it.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, to withdraw it at any time (section 12).
  • Right to grievance redressal — to have a readily available means of registering a grievance, and to have it resolved within a reasonable time (section 13).
  • Right to nominate — to nominate another individual who may exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, use the route in section 11. We will not charge you for making a request, and we will not treat you differently for exercising a right.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India in the manner provided under the DPDP Act and the Rules. We ask that you raise the matter with us first so that we have the chance to resolve it.

To request access, correction, updating, completion or erasure of your personal data, or to withdraw consent, email us at dharmanagaraidatasync@zohomail.in.

Please use the subject line "Data Request" and include:

  • the name and email address you used when you contacted us, or the role you applied for;

  • enough detail for us to locate your record — for example the approximate date of your enquiry and the interest area you selected;
  • what you want us to do, if you are asking for a correction or erasure.

We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting, using information we already hold, so that we do not disclose or delete the wrong person's data. We will not ask you to send identity documents unless it is genuinely necessary, and never through a Website form.

What happens next. We acknowledge your request, verify it, carry it out, and confirm the outcome to you. The specific acknowledgement and completion timelines we commit to are owner confirmation required — we have not stated a response time in this draft because none has been formally adopted.

If you are an employee, former employee, contractor or supplier, some requests may be governed by your contract or by employment law; we will tell you if that applies.

12. Withdrawing consent

Where we rely on your consent — responding to an enquiry submitted through a form, assessing a job application, or answering a chat message — you may withdraw that consent at any time.

You can withdraw consent by emailing us with the subject line "Withdraw Consent" at the address in section 13, or by making the same request through the route in section 11. Withdrawal is free and we will not ask you for a reason.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew it. Once consent is withdrawn, we will stop processing for that purpose and, unless we have another lawful basis or a legal obligation to keep it, erase the data.

What withdrawal means in practice. If you withdraw consent for an enquiry, we will be unable to continue corresponding with you about it. If you withdraw consent for a job application, we will stop considering it. If you withdraw consent for chat processing, you will need to stop using the AI assistant, because sending a message is the act that gives consent.

We do not send marketing communications, so there is no marketing consent to withdraw. If that changes, any marketing consent will be requested separately, will be optional, and will never be bundled with the consent needed to answer your enquiry.

16. DPDP Act and DPDP Rules — current status

We aim for this policy to describe our processing consistently with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. For accuracy, the commencement position as at the effective date of this policy is:

  • The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified by the Central Government by notification G.S.R. 846(E) dated 13 November 2025, and the DPDP Act was brought into force in phases by notification G.S.R. 843(E) of the same date.
  • From 13 November 2025 — the institutional and administrative provisions took effect, including the constitution and functioning of the Data Protection Board of India.
  • From 13 November 2026 — the Consent Manager registration framework (Rule 4) takes effect.
  • From 13 May 2027 — the principal operational obligations take effect, including the notice and consent architecture (Rule 3), security safeguards, breach notification, and the mechanism for Data Principal rights (Rules 5 to 16 and 22 to 23).

This means that some of the obligations this policy describes are not yet fully in force on the effective date of this draft, while the institutional framework — including the Board — already exists. We have drafted to the substance of the framework rather than to the current enforcement position, so that our practices do not need to be rebuilt when the remaining provisions commence. We will review this policy before 13 May 2027 and again at each change of law.

This section reflects our reading of the notifications in force on the effective date. It is not legal advice, and the commencement position should be confirmed by counsel, including any subsequent notification, corrigendum or amendment.
