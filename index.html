About Us

Where Data Meets Intelligence

Dharmanagar AI DataSync Private Limited — a technology company on a mission to simplify how businesses store, move, and understand their data.

Building the data backbone of modern businesses

Headquartered in Dharmanagar, North Tripura, DADSync helps organizations of every size harness the power of artificial intelligence and seamless data synchronization. From unified data pipelines and cloud migrations to intelligent analytics and automation, we engineer solutions that are secure, scalable, and built to last.

Our philosophy is simple: your data should work for you — never against you. We combine deep technical expertise with a founder-led, client-first approach to deliver outcomes that move the needle.
